# Grace Casa de profesores Website

## Overview
Build a polished five-page website from the supplied copy and brand guidelines, with consistent navigation, clear course pathways, and strong enquiry prompts.

## Pages
- **Home:** Full-width education-focused introduction, course summaries, benefits, activities, career support, and final call to action.
- **About Us:** Institution story, vision, mission, four-stage learning approach, student environment, and commitment.
- **Courses:** Detailed Montessori TTC and Pre-Primary TTC information, eligibility, practical training, career opportunities, and fee enquiry.
- **Why Choose Us:** Placement assistance, teaching practice, practical lab, communication and interview training, certification, faculty, campus, and activities.
- **Contact Us:** Campus details, phone/email links, office hours, enquiry form, and course links.

## Shared Experience
- Consistent desktop and mobile header with active-page navigation and enquiry action.
- Deep forest green, cream, sage, terracotta, and sand palette exactly following the supplied brand balance.
- Poppins headings and Inter body text, loaded correctly for the site.
- Professional, modern editorial layout with spacious sections, warm Montessori-inspired visual details, restrained cards, and subtle motion.
- Shared footer with all supplied contact details and page links.
- Accessible controls, keyboard-friendly mobile navigation, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.

## Visual Assets
- Create a cohesive set of warm, authentic education photographs featuring women in Montessori and early-learning settings.
- Use the strongest image as the home introduction background, with supporting images across internal pages.

## Interactions
- Course and contact actions navigate to the correct pages.
- Phone numbers and email addresses open the relevant device action.
- The enquiry form validates required details and confirms submission locally; no database or email delivery is included.

## Technical Details
- Implement each major section as its own TanStack route with unique search and social metadata.
- Define all colors, typography, shadows, and motion as reusable semantic tokens in the shared design system.
- Build reusable site header, footer, page banner, section heading, and content primitives.
- Verify the completed website at desktop and mobile sizes and resolve any visible or runtime issues.
